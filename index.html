Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart or complete the custom order form. Both options send your request to WhatsApp.

Can I request a custom cake?

Yes. Describe your preferred design, size, flavour and date in the custom order form.

Do you provide event catering?

Send us your event date, guest count, budget and preferred menu to discuss availability and pricing.

Are the prices on this website final?

No. The displayed prices are examples. Confirm current prices, quantities and delivery charges with Ese Bakery before paying.
